# Anti AI slop

Do not ship generic generated chrome. If a screen could belong to any SaaS demo, it is wrong.

Forbidden by default:

- Purple/blue gradients and glow orbs
- Three-column feature grids with icons in colored circles
- Uniform oversized border-radius on every card
- Stock-photo heroes and “Your journey starts here”
- Inter/Roboto + violet accents pretending to be a brand

Every screen needs a reason it looks like *this* product. Brand tokens and DESIGN.md win on conflict.
