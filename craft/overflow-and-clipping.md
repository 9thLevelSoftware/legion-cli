# Overflow and clipping

Do not clip content to make a mockup pretty.

- Wrap, scroll, or truncate with an affordance (ellipsis + title/tooltip). Never hide the last line of a sentence.
- Sticky headers must not cover the control they relate to.
- Modals, drawers, and toasts stay inside the viewport and can be dismissed.
- Long strings (URLs, IDs, emails) must break or scroll — they must not overflow the layout.
